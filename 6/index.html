Part 2: Training a Flow Matching Model

We now do flow matching. Also I want to note that I didn't include a part for 2.1 and 2.4 as there was no real deliverable even tho I did the parts. Instead of predicting the clean image directly, I train the UNet to predict the velocity // flow from a noisy to clean photo. From this I can denoise.

2.2 Training the Time-Conditioned UNet

Our next step, this part, is to train our UNet. I added time conditioning using FCBlocks based on the current timestep t. We randomly pick a training image and timestep (0 - 1), add noise, and then train to predict the flow/velocity at that given timestep

2.3 Sampling from the Time-Conditioned UNet

To generate images, I implemented the sampling algorithm given to us in the spec. We can see the improvements by epoch 10, the 0 is recognizable in the end.

2.5 Training the Class-Conditioned UNet

To control what digit gets generated, I added class conditioning to the UNet. Essentially, just imeplemented the algorithm on the spec. The key thing is dropping the class label randomly during training. This is important because it lets us get sharper // more recognizable numbers.

2.6 Sampling from the Class-Conditioned UNet

For sampling, I use CFG, which improves the class signal, (algorithm on spec). THis makes the generated digits much cleaner and makes it actually look like the target class. The improvement from epoch 1 to epoch 10 is pretty crazy. In epoch 10, all of the digits are recognizable (at least to me). I mean honestly even on epoch 1 I could tell but its just much more crisp.

Training without Learning Rate Scheduler
